# Majors acquired via User-session browsing; rest tiered by accessibility

For locked-down majors (Instagram, TikTok, X), we acquire content by driving the User's own logged-in sessions through browser automation — the browser-use/personal-assistant-agent pattern — rather than maintaining scrapers, proxies, or account pools. Strictly passive observation only: no likes, comments, follows, or story views, since any interaction leaves a trace visible to the Subject. Rejected alternatives: headless account pools (infrastructure-heavy, ban-farm dynamics, worst legal exposure) and long-tail-only coverage (clean but thin dossiers for typical Subjects whose presence lives on the majors). Known consequence, accepted deliberately: "no bans" is not achievable — logged-in automation is what Meta v. BrandTotal punished, and enforcement risk lands on the User's own account; human-paced, read-only, public-content-only discipline is the mitigation, not a guarantee. Everything outside the majors follows tiered-by-accessibility acquisition: full content scraping on public-friendly tiers (Reddit, GitHub, YouTube, interest sites), existence-only enumeration elsewhere, LinkedIn/Facebook skipped entirely.
